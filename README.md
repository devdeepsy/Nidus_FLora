Nidus Flora: AI-Powered Urban Gardening Companion

Welcome to **Nidus Flora**—the smart, scientific core for your beautiful green home. Designed specifically for urban apartment dwellers and modern plant parents, Nidus Flora combines advanced AI diagnostics with hyper-local lifestyle solutions to keep your indoor and balcony plants thriving.
---

Features Inside the Beta
1. AI Disease Detection: Take or upload a photo of a sick leaf to get an immediate diagnosis of common plant pests and fungi.
2. Expert AI Chatbot: A dedicated conversational assistant to help you troubleshoot watering schedules, soil mixtures, and           lighting.
3. Plant Community: A space to share photos of your apartment setups and view gardening layouts from other users.
4. Plant Care Calendar: A tool to track your routines and set customized schedules for watering and fertilizing.
5. Local Weather Tracker: Displays real-time atmospheric data so you can plan when to move plants indoors or outdoors.
6. Nidus Store: A curated storefront layout featuring essential tools, organic soil mixes, and fertilizer recommendations(Right      now it launches to the amazon app or flipkart).

*Note: As this is an early v1.0.0-beta build, these features currently operate independently as individual utility modules while I will fine-tune my core databases and API connections.*

How to Install (Android APK)
Since I am operating on a student-funded budget, the app is hosted directly via this repository for beta testers:

1. Click on the **[Releases](https://github.com)** tab on the right side of this repository page.
2. Download the latest `Nidus_Flora_Beta.apk` file to your mobile device.
3. Open the file on your smartphone and select **Install** (You may need to toggle "Allow installation from unknown sources" in your Android security settings).
